A little about what I do.

I'm Robert, and I run RB Media. I work with businesses that need a hand with their design, websites and digital projects. Sometimes that means starting from scratch. Other times, it's fixing something that's not working, updating an existing website or helping get a new idea off the ground. I like to understand what you're trying to achieve before jumping into the work, so the solution makes sense for your business and works in practice. And because you deal directly with me, there's no passing messages between departments or wondering who's looking after your project.

Branding & Design

Your brand is often the first thing people notice. I can help with logo design and visual identity, brand guidelines, company profiles and brochures, presentations, marketing materials, email signatures, banners and social media graphics.

From a single design to a more complete brand identity, the aim is to give your business a consistent look and feel.

Websites & Digital

A website should make life easier, not harder. Whether you're looking for a new website or need some help with the one you already have, I can help with website design and development, WordPress, landing pages, content updates, improvements, hosting and domain assistance, maintenance and technical support.

And once your website is live, I can help keep it up to date and running smoothly.

E-commerce Solutions

Thinking about selling online? An online shop involves more than adding products to a website. There's the catalogue, product information, search, payments, delivery and everything that happens behind the scenes.

I can help with online shop setup, product catalogues and categories, search and filtering, product or machine compatibility, payment gateways, shipping, store administration, testing and handover.

The goal is to make buying straightforward for your customers and day-to-day management manageable for you.

Digital Marketing

Not every business needs a complicated marketing campaign. Sometimes, a well-planned email or a clear message at the right time is a good place to start.

I can help with email marketing setup, Mailchimp configuration, newsletter design, email campaign content and artwork, website content and supporting graphics for digital campaigns.

Ongoing Support

The work doesn't always stop when a project is finished. Websites need updating, designs change and things occasionally stop working.

I provide ongoing help with website updates and maintenance, design and artwork changes, content updates, technical troubleshooting, hosting and domain assistance, and general website and digital support.

Sometimes you just need someone who knows your business and can help get things sorted.
